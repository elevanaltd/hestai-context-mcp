Decision: Remove the governance auto-ratify mechanism from hestai-context-mcp.

Context: The repository carried a "Governance Auto-Ratify" GitHub Actions workflow (.github/workflows/governance-ratify.yml) backed by scripts/ratify_decision.py. When a human submitted an "Approve" review on a branch whose name began with "governance/", the workflow scanned the entire .hestai/decisions/ directory and flipped every decision record carrying STATUS::PROPOSED to STATUS::RATIFIED, stamping RATIFIED_BY and RATIFIED_AT, and committed the result to the PR branch.

Decision: We remove that mechanism entirely — the workflow, the script, and its unit test. Ratifying a decision record becomes a manual, deliberate human act: an operator flips STATUS::PROPOSED to STATUS::RATIFIED and stamps RATIFIED_BY and RATIFIED_AT on the specific record being ratified, inside a normal reviewed pull request.

Rationale: The auto-ratify mechanism had two structural flaws. First, it was non-selective: because it scanned the whole decisions directory, approving a single governance pull request collaterally ratified every other pending PROPOSED decision — for example, approving the review-gate placement decision would also have ratified the unrelated centralized model-resolution decision, silently superseding a previously ratified per-repo decision. Second, it made ratification mandatory: there was no way to land a decision record that was meant to remain PROPOSED for further deliberation, because any human approval forced it to RATIFIED. Underlying both flaws is a category error — the mechanism conflated "this pull request is mergeable" with "this decision is ratified," which are distinct acts. The mechanism was introduced without a backing governance decision and was ported before its logic had been validated.

Consequences: Ratification reverts to an explicit, selective manual step. This preserves and clarifies Human Primacy (PROD I3): the human ratification act is now direct and per-record rather than an automated side effect of approving a pull request. Reintroducing any automated mutation of .hestai/decisions/ artifacts would require a new, deliberate governance decision.

Scope: This affects hestai-context-mcp governance tooling only. It does not change how decision records are authored (submit_governance) or read (lookup_decision, list_decisions, trace_supersedure).
